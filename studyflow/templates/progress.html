How completion is counted

The denominator is the requested budget of active targets in active subjects. Each completed 30-minute allocation satisfies that budget once, capped at its requested total. Missed attempts and replacements do not enlarge the denominator. Actual time includes all attempts and is never replaced by planned time. Archived records remain inspectable but are excluded from active budget totals.
